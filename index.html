EQUIPMENT

A complete professional setup, built to help you get the best out of every session.

The rig everything runs through, in the room and on the road.

  • Mac — main studio workstation
  • MacBook Pro — mobile tracking rig
  • Logic Pro & Pro Tools
  • Large-format reference display
  • Multi-channel audio interface
  • Time-aligned monitor controller

What the room hears, and what you hear back.

  • Focal nearfield monitors
  • Secondary reference monitors
  • Closed-back tracking headphones & headphone amp
  • Large-diaphragm condensers for vocals
  • Dynamic and ribbon mics for drums and amps
  • Boom stands, movable gobos and acoustic panels

Analogue in the chain where it earns its place.

  • Inline analogue mixing console
  • Valve and solid-state preamps
  • Bus compression across the mix
  • Channel-strip EQ and compression
  • Hardware reverb and delay

Enough in the room that ideas do not have to wait.

  • Acoustic drum kit & cymbals
  • MIDI keyboard controller
  • Electric and acoustic guitars
  • Bass guitar and amplification
  • Synths and drum machines
  • Percussion

Songs get finished in the right room, with the right ears in it. Every session here comes with a producer who writes, an engineer who mixes, and a setup that stays out of the way — so the only thing you have to think about is the record.

WHO YOU’RE
WORKING WITH

I’m Daniel Brazil — a record producer, songwriter and mix engineer. Most of what I do starts in the room with the artist: finding the idea, writing it properly, then producing and mixing it until it sounds like the thing we heard at the beginning.

Recent work includes co-writing and producing Gerron’s debut EP CHOKE!, which the press described as “shimmering synth textures, bright pop production and intricate vocal arrangements”. If you want a producer who will also help you write it, that’s the job.
